What changed in v0.2

RemoteBase now distinguishes between local purchasing power and your remote-income purchasing power. It also models stay length, accommodation style and season rather than treating a country as equally attractive all year.

  • Cost: city rent benchmarks from Numbeo plus a transparent RemoteBase day-to-day basket.
  • Purchasing power: local purchasing-power indices are shown as context; your Budget Power is calculated from your own spend ceiling.
  • Stay: current version is optimised for an EU citizen using a Croatian passport. EU free-movement and Japan short-stay rules are sourced separately.
  • Infrastructure: transparent editorial composite of public transport, international connectivity, broadband, rail/intercity infrastructure and everyday digital convenience.
  • Best period: monthly climate-comfort heuristic for long stays, not a weather forecast.
