How I enter a new programme

First 30 days: understand scope, stakeholders, decisions, dependencies and delivery reality.
Days 31–60: stabilise governance, ownership, reporting and blocker resolution.
Days 61–90: simplify, automate where useful, improve predictability and scale what works.

Remote by design

Several years of full-remote delivery shaped a simple principle: visibility should come from the operating system, not from people proving that they are online. I favour written-first coordination, explicit ownership, asynchronous status collection and live meetings for decisions and complex alignment.
